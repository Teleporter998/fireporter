Figma → Roblox Studio

Bring Figma layouts into Roblox Studio.

Fireporter reads a Figma selection and rebuilds it as native Roblox GUI instances. Connect a Roblox account to use Upload mode, which uploads your images to Roblox and links them in the export for you.

Connect Roblox account Read the privacy policy
Roblox Plugin Figma Plugin

Signing in is only needed to upload images, and gives you a connection code to paste into the Figma plugin. Your layout data never leaves the plugin.

01

Select in Figma

Choose a frame or layer group. Fireporter reads layout, fills, text, and effects. Turn on Preview Roblox UI to see how it will look in Studio before you export.

02

Export, and upload images if you need them

Copy the export as JSON. Sign in with Roblox and switch to Upload mode to have your images uploaded and their asset IDs filled in for you.

03

Build in Studio

Paste the JSON into the Roblox Studio plugin and it creates matching GUI instances inside your selected ScreenGui.
