Overview

Fireporter is a tool for converting UI layouts from Figma into Roblox GUI instances. We aim to collect only what is needed for the features you use, and to avoid selling or monetizing your design data.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use advertising trackers or analytics SDKs inside the plugins.

How Fireporter works

Fireporter has two main components:

  • Figma plugin — reads the layer(s) you select in Figma (position, size, fills, effects, text, and related properties) and builds an export (JSON and, when needed, image assets).
  • Roblox Studio plugin — builds matching UI instances in your open place from export data (pasted JSON or Live Mode delivery).

Network activity

Default / paste export can be used without signing in. In that flow, processing stays on your machine until you copy JSON yourself.

Live Mode and related online features (when you choose to use them) communicate with Fireporter’s backend and, when you connect a Roblox account, with Roblox’s APIs. Typical purposes include:

  • Roblox OAuth sign-in and token refresh
  • Delivering an export so Studio can import it
  • Uploading image assets to Roblox on your behalf when Live Mode / Auto Mode requires it
  • Enforcing temporary safety lockouts after certain moderation failures

Design payloads and images you export in these modes are sent only as needed to provide that functionality. They are not used for advertising.

Data storage

  • On your device — the Figma plugin may store small preferences in Figma clientStorage (for example session hints, lockout timers, or optional “My Assets” images you upload for local reuse). That data stays in your Figma client storage quota.
  • On our backend — when you use online features, we may temporarily store export payloads, session/token metadata needed for Roblox OAuth, and short-lived lockout records. Export staging data is time-limited and not kept as a permanent archive of your designs.
  • Roblox — images uploaded through Live/Auto Mode are subject to Roblox’s own storage, moderation, and policies.

Accounts & tokens

Optional Roblox login uses Roblox OAuth. Access and refresh tokens are stored so Live Mode can act on your behalf (for example uploading assets). You can log out from the plugin; logging out clears the local session association used by Fireporter.

Fireporter does not ask for your Figma password. It uses the normal Figma plugin APIs available inside the editor.

Images & moderation

When images are uploaded to Roblox, they are reviewed under Roblox’s rules. If content is rejected or an account is restricted for upload-related reasons, Fireporter may show an error and may apply a temporary lockout so the feature cannot be abused.

You are responsible for ensuring that designs and assets you export are appropriate and that you have the rights to use them.

Third-party services

Depending on the features you use, Fireporter may rely on:

  • Figma — plugin host and client storage
  • Roblox — OAuth, asset upload, and Studio
  • Hosting providers — for the Fireporter backend (e.g. deployment and short-lived data stores)

Those providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies when you use the related platforms.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the Fireporter Discord community or other support channels listed in the plugin (Socials / Support tabs).
