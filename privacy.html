Overview

Fireporter is a tool for converting UI layouts from Figma into Roblox GUI instances. We aim to collect only what is needed for the features you use, and to avoid selling or monetizing your design data.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use advertising trackers or analytics SDKs inside the plugins.

How Fireporter works

Fireporter has two main components:

  • Figma plugin — reads the layer(s) you select in Figma (position, size, fills, effects, text, and related properties) and builds an export (JSON and, when needed, image assets).
  • Roblox Studio plugin — builds matching UI instances in your open place from the JSON you paste into it. It does not contact Fireporter's backend.

Network activity

Default / paste export can be used without signing in. In that flow, processing stays on your machine until you copy JSON yourself.

Upload mode and Auto Mode (when you are signed in) communicate with Fireporter's backend and with Roblox's APIs. Typical purposes include:

  • Roblox OAuth sign-in and token refresh
  • Sending the images from your selection to Fireporter's backend, which uploads them to Roblox on your behalf and returns the asset IDs

Preview Roblox UI runs entirely inside the Figma plugin on your machine. It sends nothing to Fireporter's backend.

The Fireporter website shows the plugin's public Users, Likes, and Saves counts. To get them, the website asks Fireporter's backend, which reads those public numbers from the plugin's Figma Community page. That request contains no personal data, and visitors are not tracked.

Only those images and your connection code are sent. The rest of your design data (layout, text, colors, and structure) stays in the plugin and is never sent to Fireporter's backend. Images are used only to provide the upload and are not used for advertising.

Data storage

  • On your device — the Figma plugin stores small items in Figma clientStorage: your Roblox connection code, your username, simple preferences, and any optional "My Assets" images you upload for local reuse. That data stays in your Figma client storage quota.
  • On our backend — Fireporter's backend has no database. It does not store your designs, images, tokens, or account details. Requests are processed in memory and discarded when they finish. Our hosting provider may keep standard operational logs, such as request times and error messages, under its own policy.
  • Roblox — images uploaded through Upload or Auto Mode are subject to Roblox's own storage, moderation, and policies.

Accounts & tokens

Optional Roblox login uses Roblox OAuth. Fireporter asks for permission to read your basic profile, read and write your assets, and read your inventory items, so that it can upload images to your account. After you approve, the backend bundles your Roblox access and refresh tokens, user ID, and username into a connection code, encrypts it with a key that only the backend holds, and shows it to you on the confirmation page. You paste it into the plugin, which keeps it in Figma clientStorage on your device. The plugin sends it back with each upload so the backend can act on your behalf. Fireporter keeps no copy.

Logging out removes the code from the plugin. Because Fireporter keeps no server-side record, you can also revoke its access at any time from your Roblox account settings. Treat the connection code like a password and do not share it.

Fireporter does not ask for your Figma password. It uses the normal Figma plugin APIs available inside the editor.

Images & moderation

When images are uploaded to Roblox, they are reviewed under Roblox's rules. If content is rejected or an account is restricted for upload-related reasons, Fireporter shows an error for the affected images, and the rest of your export still completes.

You are responsible for ensuring that designs and assets you export are appropriate and that you have the rights to use them.

Support tickets

The Support tab lets you open a support ticket instead of messaging on Discord. Submitting a ticket sends the following to the developer by email, for the sole purpose of responding to your request:

  • The category and subject you selected
  • The message you wrote
  • The email address you provide, so we can reply
  • Any files you choose to attach (screenshots, video, or Figma/Roblox files) — attachments are optional and only sent if you add them

Ticket content is delivered directly by email through our email-delivery provider and is not stored by Fireporter's backend. Attachment size and file type are limited on the client before anything is sent.

Third-party services

Depending on the features you use, Fireporter may rely on:

  • Figma — plugin host and client storage
  • Roblox — OAuth, asset upload, and Studio
  • Hosting providers — for the Fireporter backend (serverless deployment and standard operational logs)
  • Email delivery provider — used only to send support tickets you submit to the developer's inbox

Those providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies when you use the related platforms.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the Fireporter Discord community or other support channels listed in the plugin (Socials / Support tabs).
