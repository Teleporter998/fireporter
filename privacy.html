Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7/30/2026

Fireporter does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

How Fireporter works

Fireporter consists of two parts:

  • Figma plugin: reads the layer(s) you select inside Figma (position, size, fills, effects, text, etc.) and converts that data into a JSON export shown directly in the plugin window for you to copy.
  • Roblox Studio plugin: takes JSON you paste in and builds matching UI instances inside your currently open Roblox place.

Network activity

Fireporter does not make any network requests. It does not contact Figma's API, Roblox's API, or any third-party server. All processing happens locally within Figma or Roblox Studio, and JSON data is only transferred when you manually copy and paste it yourself.

Data storage

Fireporter does not store any data outside of your current Figma or Roblox Studio session. Once you close the plugin or clear the input, no history or record of your export/import is kept by Fireporter.

Account or API tokens

Fireporter does not require or request access to your Figma account, Figma API tokens, Roblox account, or any credentials.

Third-party services

Fireporter does not integrate with or share data with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
